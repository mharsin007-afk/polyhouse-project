\# Exploratory Data Analysis Notes



\## Correlation Analysis



A correlation heatmap was generated to analyze relationships between environmental variables and mushroom yield.



\### Key Findings



\- The strongest positive correlation was observed between Temperature and Yield (r = 0.524), indicating that higher temperatures were generally associated with higher mushroom yield.

\- The strongest negative correlation was observed between CO₂ concentration and Yield (r = -0.260), suggesting a weak inverse relationship.

\- Humidity showed a weak positive correlation with Yield (r = 0.242).



\### Scatter Plot Observations



\- Temperature vs Yield shows a moderate positive trend.

\- Humidity vs Yield shows a weak positive relationship.

\- CO₂ vs Yield shows a weak negative relationship.



\### Caveats



\- Correlation does not imply causation.

\- The observed relationships are moderate to weak and may be influenced by other environmental factors.

\- Additional statistical analysis and predictive modeling are required before drawing operational conclusions.
